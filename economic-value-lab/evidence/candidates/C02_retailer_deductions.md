# C02 — Invalid retailer deductions at mid-market consumer-goods brands (primarily US)

Status: **SURVIVOR — suspended on soft kill S1 (magnitude evidence is vendor-only).**
Evidence confidence: **M-L**. Rubric 77.4 (#2), robust.

## Mechanism

Retailers and distributors pay suppliers net of deductions (shortages, damages, compliance fines,
promotional allowances). Some deductions are invalid. Disputing needs matching of proof of
delivery, bills of lading, promotion agreements and retailer-specific portal rules within strict
windows. Small brands write them off. Mechanism class: M1.

## Estimates

`ESTIMATE` If 1–3% of wholesale revenue is invalidly deducted (vendor figure), a $10m brand loses
$100–300k/yr; at a 20–30% contingency fee a provider earns $20–90k per client per year. Not usable
for selection until corroborated by an independent source.
