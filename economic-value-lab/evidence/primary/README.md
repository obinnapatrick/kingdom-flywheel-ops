## Caveats on the documents themselves

- **P01 date and status.** The brief referred to a "July 2026" evaluation. The document supplied
  is dated **June 2025** and carries a leftover drafting note: "Draft note: Insert reference",
  footnote 18, p.68. It may be a pre-publication version. The owner should confirm it matches the
  published version.
- **P01 vs P02 inconsistencies.** Neither document resolves these:
  - Energy: the main report (p.81) presents a 56% energy-cost reduction as a finding. The annex
    (A.7.6) says there is "no overall effect on energy costs across the three models". The 56%
    effect appears only in year 3, in one model.
  - Intermediate consumption: the main report says 40–50% lower; the annex's year-3 figures are
    33% / 54% / 41%.
- **P03** is a web bulletin printed to PDF, and its URL carries a `utm_source=chatgpt.com` tag,
  so the owner reached it through a third-party tool. The content is ONS text. The scores are
  corrected figures (correction dated 5 July 2024).
- **P04** is a consultancy marketing page. It covers **listed** companies only ("more than 17,000
  listed companies"), describes no method for estimating "excess", and ends with a sales section
  ("How we can help").
